Slide 5 of 5 · Task 5

Challenges & Lessons Learned

⚡ Challenges Encountered

Non-root Container Security

Gunicorn needed a writable temp dir. Solved using an emptyDir volume mounted at /tmp.

Ansible Windows Limitation

Ansible is Linux-native. Playbooks are written for remote Linux targets, requiring WSL or a CI runner to execute.

Grafana Auto-provisioning

Required precise directory structure under /provisioning for datasource & dashboard YAML to load on startup.
